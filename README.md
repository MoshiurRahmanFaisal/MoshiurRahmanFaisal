## 👋 About Me

I'm an **Associate Manager, AI & Data Science** at **Robi Axiata PLC**, Bangladesh, where I build end-to-end ML systems for retailer analytics and geospatial site intelligence. On the research side, I work on **NLP for low-resource languages**, with a focus on Bengali and Banglish, mental health text, and fairness in language models.

- 🏢 **Now:** shipping production ML pipelines on PySpark, Hive and AWS SageMaker
- 🔬 **Researching:** cross-lingual model behaviour, mental health NLP, bias in LLMs
- 🎓 **Background:** BSc in CSE, North South University *(Cum Laude)*
- 🏆 **Recognition:** Q1 2026 CS Excellence Award, Corporate Strategy, Robi Axiata
- 🎯 **Next:** actively seeking **PhD opportunities** in NLP and responsible AI
- 🎮 **Off the clock:** Valorant. My aim is better with models than with a Vandal.
